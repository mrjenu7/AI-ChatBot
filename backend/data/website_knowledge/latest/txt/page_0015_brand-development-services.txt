Questions
Brand Development Questions We Often Hear
01
What is brand development?
−
Brand development is the complete process of building a brand: research, positioning, name and story, logo and visual identity, communication, brand guidelines and the launch. It is much more than logo design.
02
What is the difference between brand development and logo design?
+
A logo is one part of a brand. Brand development covers the strategy behind the logo, the name, the voice, the visual identity system, the stationery, the digital and packaging design, the brand book and the launch.
03
Who needs brand development services?
+
Any new business, startup or company that wants to launch a website, a mobile app or a software product as a brand, and any existing business that needs a brand refresh or rebranding.
04
What does We3vision deliver in a brand development project?
+
Depending on the package: brand research and strategy, name and tagline, logo and logo variations, typography and colours, stationery, social media templates, brand guidelines, packaging, website or app branding and a launch kit. Deliverables and revisions are written in the project scope and contract.
05
Can you do only a part of the brand?
+
Yes. We offer a starter, a complete and a premium brand package, and individual services such as brand strategy, logo design, brand kit design, packaging or a brand launch.
06
Do you also build the website or app for my brand?
+
Yes. We3vision is a design and development company, so we can brand your product and also build the website, mobile app or software that carries the brand.
07
Do you work on rebranding?
+
Yes. We audit your existing brand and then refresh the logo, identity, messaging, packaging, website and guidelines, with a plan to introduce the change.
08
Do you offer social media management?
+
Our brand development includes social media branding: templates, profile design and guidelines. Daily posting, content calendars and community management are offered as a separate service.
