11
Frequently Asked
Questions.
What services are included in physical marketing?
−
Design and planning for print, outdoor, retail, event, promotional and activation materials, so your brand shows up in the real world and connects with your digital marketing.
Do you design brochures, posters and catalogues?
+
Yes. We design them and prepare print-ready artwork, with specifications for the printer.
Can you help with billboard and outdoor advertising?
+
We can plan the format and design the artwork. Media space, permissions and installation come from media owners and production partners, which we can coordinate where it is in the scope.
Do you manage event and exhibition branding?
+
Yes. We can design booth and backdrop graphics, signage, handouts and a follow-up flow to capture leads.
Is printing included in your service?
+
Design and artwork are ours. Printing and production depend on the approved scope and on vendor arrangements, and can be coordinated if included.
Can physical campaigns connect to digital marketing?
+
Yes. QR codes, short URLs, dedicated landing pages and campaign tags can link a physical piece to an online action.
Can you coordinate production and installation?
+
Where it is part of the scope, we coordinate with printers, fabricators and installers. This is agreed in the proposal.
How is the cost of a physical campaign calculated?
+
It depends on the formats, quantities, materials, locations, production and installation requirements. We prepare a tailored proposal after we understand the brief.
