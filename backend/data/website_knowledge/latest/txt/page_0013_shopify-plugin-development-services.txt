FAQ
Questions
We
Often
Hear
Tap a card to turn it over
01
What is the difference between a Shopify plugin and a Shopify app?
Answer
People often use both words for the same thing. A plugin usually means a small add-on feature for your store, such as a theme extension or section. A Shopify app can be larger, has its own admin screens and can serve many stores. We help you pick the lighter option that does the job.
02
Can you build a plugin for my existing theme?
Answer
Yes. We build plugins for most themes, including Online Store 2.0 themes, and test them on a duplicate of your theme first.
03
Will a custom plugin slow down my store?
Answer
A custom plugin carries only the code it needs, so it is usually lighter than a ready-made app. We also check the speed of your store before and after.
04
Can my team manage the plugin without a developer?
Answer
Yes. We add settings to the theme editor so that your team can change text, colours and where the plugin appears.
05
Can you fix or upgrade a plugin that already exists?
Answer
Yes. We review the code, repair what is broken, update it for current Shopify versions and improve its speed.
06
How long does a Shopify plugin take to build?
Answer
A small plugin can take a few days, while larger ones with settings and integrations take longer. We give you a clear timeline after we understand what you need.
