08
what do the numbers in my ads report mean
Understand What Your Campaign
Is Actually Delivering.
The columns we report, and how to read each. Results vary with offer, audience, budget, creative, competition and website. No sample dashboard is shown, because nothing here is live.
Reports · columns
Concept
Metric
What it shows
Read it with care
Reach and impressions
How many people saw the ad, and how many times it was shown.
A large reach is not the same as interest.
Frequency
How often the average person saw the ad.
Too high can mean fatigue, too low may mean the message is not remembered.
Link clicks
How many people clicked through to your site or app.
Clicks do not tell you whether they found what they wanted.
Landing-page views
How many clicks became real page visits.
A big gap against clicks can point to slow pages or tracking problems.
Cost per click
How much, on average, each click cost.
A cheap click is not always a good one.
Leads or purchases
The valuable actions people took, as recorded by your tracking.
Depends on correct tracking and on lead quality.
Cost per result
What each lead or purchase cost, on average.
Compare it with what a result is worth to you.
Conversion rate
The share of visitors who completed the action.
It reflects the offer and the website as much as the ad.
Return on ad spend
Revenue linked to the ads compared with what was spent.
Only meaningful where sales are reliably tracked.

10
meta marketing questions
Frequently Asked
Questions.
What is Meta marketing?
−
It is advertising and promotion on Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram: planning the campaign, creating the ads, choosing audiences, running the campaign and measuring what it delivers.
Do you manage Facebook and Instagram ads?
+
Yes. We set up, run, monitor and optimize campaigns, and report on them clearly.
Do you create ad creatives?
+
Yes. Static images, carousels, short videos, reels-style content and motion graphics, with variations for testing.
Can you help generate leads?
+
Yes. We can plan lead campaigns with instant forms or landing pages and help connect them to your team or CRM. We cannot promise a number of leads.
Is the advertising budget included in your service fee?
+
No. Media spend is paid to the platform and our fee is separate, unless a proposal clearly says otherwise.
Can you set up Pixel and conversion tracking?
+
Yes. We can support Meta Pixel and Conversions API set-up and check events, following privacy, consent and platform rules.
Do you guarantee leads or sales?
+
No. Results depend on the offer, audience, budget, creative, competition and your website, so we do not guarantee leads or sales.
Can you manage existing ad accounts?
+
Yes. We can review an existing account and campaigns, then improve the structure, creative and tracking.
