FAQ
Questions
We
Often
Hear
Q
What is included in brand video production?
Planning, scripting, shooting, editing and delivery. We handle the whole process so you receive finished videos that are ready to publish.
Q
Do you work with influencers?
Yes. We arrange and brief suitable influencers, plan the shoot together and edit the final content so it fits your brand.
Q
Can you shoot and edit in one package?
Yes. Planning, shooting and editing are all handled by our team.
Q
How long does a brand video take?
It depends on the length and the shoot. A short reel can be ready in days, and a brand film takes a few weeks. We share a schedule before we begin.
Q
Can you make videos for Instagram and YouTube?
Yes. We deliver the same video in different sizes and lengths for each platform.
Q
Do I need to prepare a script?
No. We help with the idea, script and storyboard. If you already have a script, we can work with it.
Q
Where do you shoot?
We shoot at your location, in a studio or at a chosen outdoor location, depending on the video. We are based in Surat, India and can plan shoots in other cities.
