01
What is 3D modeling?
3D modeling is the process of creating digital three-dimensional objects or environments. These can be used for product visualization, animation, games, immersive experiences and other digital applications.
02
What types of 3D modeling services does We3vision offer?
We3vision's listed 3D services include 3D development, 3D product visualization, 3D modeling, 3D animation and interactive 3D experiences. The specific deliverables depend on your project requirements.
03
Can you create custom 3D product models?
Yes, custom 3D product modeling can be discussed based on your product references, design requirements and intended use. Product complexity, formats and deliverables are confirmed before starting the project.
04
Can 3D models be used in AR, VR or metaverse projects?
Yes. 3D models can be used as assets in AR, VR and metaverse experiences. The modeling and optimization requirements are determined by the target platform and the planned interactions.
05
What information do you need to start a 3D modeling project?
Share your project concept, reference images, product specifications, preferred visual style, intended platform and expected deliverables. Our team can then discuss the scope and requirements.
06
How long does a 3D modeling project take?
The timeline depends on the complexity of the model, level of detail, number of assets, revisions and project requirements. We3vision can provide it after reviewing your project scope.
07
Can you optimize 3D models for interactive experiences?
3D asset optimization can be considered when the project needs performance for interactive or immersive platforms. The approach depends on the target device and application.
08
How much do 3D modeling services cost?
The cost depends on the complexity of the model, design requirements, number of assets, texturing, revisions and final deliverables. Contact We3vision for a project-specific estimate.
