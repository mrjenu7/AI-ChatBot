Frequently Asked
Questions.
What is included in end-to-end brand development?
−
It can include research, strategy, naming, logo design, visual identity, brand assets, guidelines and launch materials, depending on the agreed scope.
Can I choose only one branding service?
+
Yes. Individual services such as Brand Design, Brand Kit Design and Brand Video can also be commissioned separately.
Can you rebrand an existing business?
+
Yes. We can assess your current identity and develop a refreshed brand strategy, visual system and rollout plan.
Do you provide website and packaging design?
+
Yes. Website, app and packaging design can be included according to your business needs and project scope.
Will I receive the source files?
+
Deliverable formats, editable files and source assets will be specified in the project proposal.
How much does brand development cost?
+
Pricing depends on the scope, number of deliverables, complexity and timeline. Contact our team for a tailored proposal.
