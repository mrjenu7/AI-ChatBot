seo and aeo questions
Frequently Asked
Questions.
People also ask
What is the difference between SEO and AEO?
−
SEO helps search engines find, understand and rank your pages. AEO shapes the same content so that clear, well-supported answers are easier for answer systems to interpret and possibly surface. They overlap heavily: helpful content and a healthy website help both.
How long can SEO take to show results?
+
It depends on the competition, the condition of the site, the quality of the content and how often changes are made. Some technical fixes show effects sooner, while content and authority usually take longer to build. We do not promise a fixed timeline.
Can you optimize an existing website?
+
Yes. We start with an assessment of the current site, then fix what holds it back and improve what already works, without rebuilding it unless that is really needed.
Do you provide technical SEO and content SEO?
+
Yes. Technical SEO, on-page work, keyword research, content strategy and optimization, local SEO and AEO are all part of our scope, depending on what your site needs.
Can you help improve AI search visibility?
+
We can help you make your information clearer, more consistent and easier to crawl, which can make it more eligible to be used in AI-generated answers. No one can guarantee inclusion in them.
How is SEO progress measured?
+
Where tracking is set up, we look at organic clicks and impressions, query performance, indexed pages, landing-page engagement and relevant conversions, and we keep activity metrics separate from business outcomes.
Do you guarantee search rankings?
+
No. Rankings depend on competition, technical condition, content quality, authority and many other factors, some of which we do not control. We commit to careful, ethical work and clear reporting, not to a ranking position.
