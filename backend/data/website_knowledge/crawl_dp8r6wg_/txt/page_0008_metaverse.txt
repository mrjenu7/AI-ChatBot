Augmented Reality (AR)
Augmented Reality
(AR)
Digital layers on top of the real world: product previews, interactive guides and try-ons on a phone or tablet.
Right for you when
You want people to see and try something in their own space, from a phone, without a headset.
Problems it solves
Customers cannot picture a product in their own space
A flat catalogue that does not show the product
Guides and manuals that are hard to follow
What you get
Products shown in the real world through a phone camera
Interactive guides and visual explanations
An easy first step into immersive technology

Virtual Reality (VR)
Virtual Reality
(VR)
Fully immersive virtual worlds, showrooms and training that people step into with a headset.
Right for you when
The experience needs people to feel inside a place: a showroom, a site, a training room or an event.
Problems it solves
Places that are far away, unbuilt or costly to visit
Training that is hard or risky to practise for real
A presentation that does not leave an impression
What you get
A virtual space people can walk through
Safe, repeatable training and walkthroughs
A memorable experience for customers and teams

Extended Reality (XR)
Extended Reality
(XR)
The umbrella for AR, VR and MR: immersive experiences planned across devices and platforms.
Right for you when
You are not sure which immersive technology suits you, or you want one experience that works on several devices.
Problems it solves
Unclear which technology fits the goal
Separate builds for every device
No plan from idea to launch
What you get
A clear choice between AR, VR and MR for your goal
One plan across devices and platforms
A roadmap from concept to launch

Mixed Reality (MR)
Mixed Reality
(MR)
Virtual objects that sit in the real space around you and respond to it: demos, visualisation and teamwork.
Right for you when
Digital content has to live in the real room and react to it: product demos, design reviews or shared work.

Design reviews on flat screens
Remote teams that cannot look at the same thing

Virtual objects placed in real space
Better reviews and demos of products and spaces

FAQ
Questions
We Often Hear
What are metaverse solutions?
Metaverse solutions are interactive digital experiences that may include virtual worlds, metaverse applications, virtual events and immersive environments.
What metaverse services does We3vision offer?
Our listed services include metaverse development, metaverse applications, virtual world development and virtual events and experiences.
Can We3vision develop a custom virtual world?
We3vision offers virtual world development as part of its metaverse service portfolio. Project features and implementation requirements can be discussed during the planning stage.
Can metaverse solutions support virtual events?
Yes. Our listed services include virtual events and experiences for businesses exploring interactive digital gatherings.
Can metaverse experiences include 3D environments?
Yes. Metaverse projects can be planned alongside our 3D development, 3D modeling and interactive 3D experience services.
Which industries can use metaverse solutions?
Metaverse experiences can be explored for education, retail, gaming, real estate, marketing, corporate events and other suitable use cases.
How long does metaverse development take?
The timeline depends on the project scope, virtual environment complexity, features, 3D assets and required integrations.
How much does metaverse development cost?
The cost depends on the type of experience, design requirements, functionality, 3D development needs and deployment environment.
Can you help us plan a metaverse project?
Yes. We can discuss your business goals, target users, virtual experience concept and project requirements to plan the next steps.
How can I start a metaverse project with We3vision?
Contact We3vision with your project idea, business objectives and preferred virtual experience. Our team can review your requirements and discuss a suitable development approach.
