Why the team matters
Behind
every
screen,
there
is
a
person
A website, an app or a virtual world looks like technology. It is really a long list of small decisions, made by people who care.
The way a button feels, the way a page loads, the way a 3D model moves:
somebody thought about every one of them.
At We3vision that somebody sits in our office in Surat. They are developers, designers, 3D artists, engineers and marketers, and they have been building together since 2019. We do not hide them behind a company name. This page is here to introduce them.
Tools change every year. What does not change is that good work comes from people who listen, disagree kindly, help each other and finish what they start. That is why the team comes first at We3vision, and the projects come after.
We move forward because they move forward. Every project we ship is a thank-you to the people who built it.

How we work
How
a
project
moves
through
the
team
Nobody builds alone here. A project passes from hand to hand like a relay race, and every hand matters.
01
We listen
Business development
We start with your goal, your users and your limits. Questions first, ideas second.
02
We plan
Project managers
The idea becomes a clear plan with steps, dates and one person you can always ask.
03
We design
UI/UX and brand designers
Screens, logos and 3D worlds take shape. You see them early and tell us how they feel.
04
We build
Web, mobile, AI and metaverse developers
Developers and engineers turn the design into something that really works, on web, mobile, AI or VR.
05
We test
QA engineers
Somebody who did not build it tries to break it. What breaks is fixed before you ever see it.
06
We launch and care
The whole team
We go live together, and then we stay: updates, fixes and growth with SEO and marketing.
Our values
What
we
believe
in
as
a
team
Not words on a wall. These are the habits you will see in our office every day.
01
People before projects
A project ends, a person stays. We look after the team first, because a team that is looked after looks after its clients.
02
Together, not one after the other
Designers, developers and artists work on the same problem at the same time. The best idea wins, whoever it came from.
03
Own it from start to finish
Everyone owns something real: a screen, a module, a client. If it is yours, you finish it and you fix it.
04
Keep learning
AR, VR, AI and new frameworks: the field moves fast, so learning is part of the work, not a favour.
05
Be honest, and be kind
We tell clients what is possible and what is not. We tell each other what works and what does not.
06
Make it simple
Clear layouts, clear words, clear promises. If a user has to think twice, we are not done.
Growing together
How
we
help
the
people
grow
A company moves forward only when its people do. This is what we try to give everybody who joins us.
01
Real projects from the first weeks
New people work on live projects with a mentor next to them, not on practice tasks.
02
Mentors in every team
Senior people, our founders among them, guide the younger ones, in the metaverse team and beyond.
03
Room to go deep
Web, mobile, AI, VR, 3D, brand or marketing: you can grow in the field you love.
04
Credit where it is due
Good work is named and shown, on this page and on our projects.
05
A team that helps
Stuck? Ask. Somebody in the office has been there before and will sit down with you.
06
A path upward
People grow with the company, from their first project to leading one.

Questions
about

Still have a question?
Tell us what you need and we will answer you ourselves.
Talk to us →
01
Where is the We3vision team based?
Our head office and development team are in Surat, Gujarat, India. We also have an office in Marburg, Germany.
02
Who will I talk to when I start a project?
You get one main contact, a project manager, who knows everything about your project. Designers and developers join the calls whenever it helps.
03
What kind of people work at We3vision?
Web and mobile developers, UI/UX and graphic designers, 2D/3D animators, AR/VR and Unity engineers, AI engineers, QA engineers, SEO specialists and content writers.
04
Can I join the team?
Yes. Look at the open roles on our Careers page, or write to hr@we3vision.com with your work and a few words about you.
05
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes. We work with companies across India and the globe, and we have an office in Germany.
